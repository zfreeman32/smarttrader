## Scope decision

- The next collection is research-only. Every selected model runs in shadow mode, writes simulated outcomes, and has no broker-order, fill, position, paper-trial, or alert authority.
- FRVP and ICT are the ES stream. The retained OTE models were trained from `eurusd_5min_ote_full`; they must run as a parallel EURUSD stream under the same research program, never on ES bars. Keep ES ticks/USD and EURUSD pips in separate ledgers and never sum or rank them as one P&L series.
- Pooled FRVP, ICT, and OTE models may be included for this collection. This requires a new versioned research scope because locked `baseline_scope_v1` makes pooled models diagnostic-only. Do not rewrite or weaken the archived B1 scope.
- Historical profitability, Sharpe/DSR, WFE, drawdown, concentration, cadence, AUC/AP lift, and setup-only outperformance are advisory roster-ranking evidence for this no-order collection. A promising or diversifying model may collect despite failing them.
- The hard collection gates are: exact live-path feature parity, causal availability, complete trading/outcome rules, realistic costs, immutable version bindings, attributable logging, deterministic replay, acceptable measured latency, and end-to-end readiness.
- These relaxed research gates do not relax promotion-to-trading gates. No shadow result is a paper/live promotion without a later, separate decision.

### Shadow-collection expansion decision

Broaden the research shadow collection without broadening promotion eligibility. Use two explicit namespaces:

- **Scored research shadow:** repaired, parity-verified, fully bound models with independent outcome and position ledgers.
- **Diagnostic shadow:** legacy or weak models that still emit raw scores, setup links, rejection reasons, overlap identity and forward markouts, but have no P&L, promotion, paper, alert or order authority.

Add or prioritize these companions after the current core is technically ready:

- **ID 5 — FRVP long S2:** now included in the intended 16-model staged roster. Its setup-specific run had attractive expectancy/PF and lower drawdown, but failed WFE and concentration. Collect beside FRVP continuation/meta/reversal as a setup-diversity comparison.
- **ID 23 — OTE long Union:** now included in the intended 16-model staged roster after corrected OTE timestamp and live-parity checks. Keep it EURUSD-only and diagnostic because pooled/union outputs are not promotion candidates. ID 27 remains reserve.
- **ID 18 — ICT short IFVG reversal:** now included as a paired diagnostic with ID 19 sweep/reclaim and ID 16 reversal. IFVG has classifier signal without realized edge, while sweep/reclaim is positive but not promotion-cleared; the pair can test whether the apparent edge is additive or merely a shared subset.
- **ID 14 — ICT short continuation:** now included as a low-cost companion for IDs 15/16. It shares exact events with short-meta while its unique remainder was negative, making it useful to test whether it contributes conditional information rather than as an independent strategy.

Do not add FRVP S4, FRVP short reversal, FRVP S6, OTE breakouts, classic breaker, missing-history/native-1m models, or extremely thin ICT setup branches to the launch collection. Retain thin setup events as event-only diagnostics where useful.

For every selected model, retain raw scores below threshold, same-opportunity pair states (`both`, `A_only`, `B_only`, `neither`), exact overlap identity, independent outcomes and rejected-opportunity outcomes. Do not let one model filter another during the baseline. Predeclare ensemble, routing or disagreement experiments only after the common baseline is collected.

If one genuinely new model is built after the baseline, prioritize an out-of-fold XGB+TCN ensemble over another same-family model. Collect the raw component-pair data first and evaluate the ensemble on a later frozen sample.
